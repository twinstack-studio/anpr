How it works

  1. Vehicle detection & tracking. A YOLO11 model finds cars, bikes, buses and trucks in every frame. ByteTrack gives each vehicle an ID that follows it through the clip.
  2. Plate detection, trained by us. A second YOLO11 model, fine-tuned on 1,578 photos of Pakistani number plates (Punjab, Sindh, Islamabad, KP), looks for the plate inside each vehicle. On held-out photos it scores 100% precision and 97.4% recall (mAP50 97.5%).
  3. Plate reading (OCR), trained by us. A compact vision-transformer OCR model, fine-tuned on 1,471 hand-checked Pakistani plate crops, reads the characters on the GPU. On vehicles it never saw in training it reads the full plate correctly 88% of the time (79% before our training), with 97% of characters correct.
  4. Pakistani plate rules. Readings are cleaned for local formats (LEB-1234, BKA-123, LEA-20-4060, LE-16-5471A): letters and digits that look alike (0/O, 8/B, 5/S) are fixed based on their position on the plate.
  5. Voting over frames. A plate is read many times while the vehicle passes. The readings are combined, weighted by confidence and plate size, so one blurry frame does not decide the result. Weak or one-off readings are dropped.
  6. Business logic. Each vehicle is logged with time, gate, direction and crops. Entry and exit clips become parking sessions with fees, and watchlist plates raise alerts.
